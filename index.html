About The Company

Committed to Quality.
Driven by Scale.

Operating from our modern, state-of-the-art facility at 300/6 IMT Manesar, Zooto Fashion has developed and implemented a quality management system according to European Standards. We are a fully compliant factory authorized by leading agencies and the Govt of India.

Our Vision

To develop ZootoFashion as a well-managed company matching International Standards, maintaining Integrity, High Ethical Standards, and Transparency in our system. Providing an environment of professionalism, competence, teamwork, and customer service excellence.

Our Mission

To bring quality footwear of international standards, comparable with global shoemakers. We are committed to achieving excellence in shoemaking for the benefit of our global customers.

About The Company

Committed to Quality.
Driven by Scale.

Zooto Fashion Pvt Ltd is an ambitious industrial venture engaged in the manufacturing, importing and exporting of footwear and footwear components of the best quality since 2022.

Our modern manufacturing facility is located at 300/6, IMT Manesar with an installed capacity of 1 Lakh pairs per month. We are a fully compliant factory recognised by leading agencies and the Govt of India, and have implemented a quality management system according to European Standards.

We are committed to improving our environmental status by providing a safe and healthy working environment to all people involved in the manufacturing of our products.

Our Vision

To develop Zooto Fashion as a well-managed company matching International Standards, maintaining Integrity, High Ethical Standards, and Transparency in our system — providing an environment of professionalism, competence, teamwork and customer-service excellence.

Our Mission

To bring quality footwear of international standards, comparable with global shoemakers. We are committed to achieving excellence in shoemaking for the benefit of our global customers.

FAQs

Questions, Answered.

What footwear does Zooto Fashion manufacture?
We manufacture joggers, sneakers, runners, sliders, clogs (including kids' clogs) and new EVA moulds — engineered to European quality standards for global fashion and lifestyle brands.
What is your production capacity?
Our IMT Manesar facility has an installed capacity of 1 lakh pairs per month, across a 44,000 sq ft production site backed by an advanced ERP and inventory system.
Do you offer OEM & private-label manufacturing?
Yes. We provide full OEM and private-label manufacturing with in-house design support, quick prototyping, and custom development to your specifications.
Are you a compliant factory?
We are a fully compliant, Govt. of India recognised unit and Sedex audit compliant, with a quality management system aligned to European Standards.
